# Kiana Mahtabi Nourani

### Interdisciplinary AI & Computational Biology Researcher

**MSc Candidate in Electrical & Electronic Engineering | Eastern Mediterranean University**

I am an MSc candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Eastern Mediterranean University, with an undergraduate background in Biomedical Engineering.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational biology, and systems engineering, with a particular focus on developing computational approaches for biological data analysis, disease modeling, and AI-assisted therapeutic design.

## Selected Research Projects

### RFdiffusion-Based De Novo Therapeutic Antibody Design Against Malaria
**MSc Thesis | Eastern Mediterranean University | Ongoing**

Developing a computational framework integrating transcriptomic analysis, protein structural characterization, and generative AI for candidate antibody design targeting *Plasmodium falciparum* proteins.

**Research methods:** Transcriptomic analysis, target prioritization, protein surface accessibility assessment, evolutionary conservation analysis, candidate epitope identification, and RFdiffusion-based antibody design.

### A Systems-Level In Silico Framework for Machine Learning-Based Modeling of Splice-Modifying Drug Responses in Spinal Muscular Atrophy
**IEEE HONET-ICT | Accepted**

Investigated computational approaches for modeling splice-modifying drug responses in spinal muscular atrophy using gene expression and RNA splicing information, systems-level analysis, and machine learning.

**Research methods:** RNA splicing analysis, systems-level computational modeling, feature analysis, and machine learning-based drug-response prediction.

### Graph-to-Image Representation Framework for Modeling SMN2 Splice-Site Responses
**Research Project**

Developed a graph-to-image representation framework to investigate computational representations of splice-site responses to therapeutic compounds in spinal muscular atrophy.

**Research methods:** Graph-based representation, graph-to-image transformation, dimensionality reduction, and predictive modeling.

### Systems-Level Biomarker Discovery in Spinal Muscular Atrophy
**BATS 2024 | Published Research**

Investigated disease-associated molecular biomarkers through computational gene expression analysis and systems-level approaches.

**Research methods:** Gene expression analysis, biomarker prioritization, clustering, and computational systems biology.

## Featured Research Repositories

### [SMA Splicing Drug Response ML](https://github.com/kianamahtabinourani/SMA-splicing-drug-response-ML)

A computational machine learning framework for modeling splice-modifying drug responses in spinal muscular atrophy (SMA). Integrates RNA splicing measurements, sequence-regulatory feature engineering, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling to investigate drug-induced changes in exon inclusion.

**Methods & Tools:** MATLAB · Machine Learning · RNA Splicing · Feature Engineering · Statistical Analysis · Computational Genomics

**Research Publication:** IEEE HONET-ICT 2025 (Accepted)

### [SMA Biomarker Discovery and Clustering | BATS 2024](https://github.com/kianamahtabinourani/SMA-biomarker-clustering)

A systems-level computational framework for investigating gene expression patterns and identifying potential biomarkers associated with spinal muscular atrophy (SMA). Integrates machine learning, gene expression analysis, and clustering techniques to explore disease-associated molecular signatures.

**Methods & Tools:** MATLAB · Machine Learning · Gene Expression Analysis · Clustering · Systems Biology

**Research Publication:** BATS 2024, Rome, Italy (Published)

### [Malaria Gene Expression Network](https://github.com/kianamahtabinourani/malaria-gene-expression-network)

Network-based computational analysis of malaria gene expression data to investigate gene associations and biological network structure.

**Methods & Tools:** MATLAB · Gene Expression Networks · Network Analysis · Computational Biology
